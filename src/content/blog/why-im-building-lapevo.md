## Background

In my free time, when I'm not working on other projects or working, I enjoy driving in, and racing other people on, iRacing.

As I've gotten into it more, I started using tools like **Garage61** to analyze my telemetry and find ways to drive faster and more consistently.
This has been very useful to me as it gives me a data-driven method to analyze my past performance and identify patterns in my driving style that can be improved. However, a significant shortcoming of this process is that you can only analyze your data post-session and theres no easy way to get feedback during your driving sessions without paying for proprietary tools like **Trophi.ai**.
